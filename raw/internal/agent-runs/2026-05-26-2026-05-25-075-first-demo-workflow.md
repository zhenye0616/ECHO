## Decisions made during implementation

1. **No `src/echo-home/index.ts` export.** `syncDefaultWorkflows()` is consumed internally by `syncAll()` and directly by its own unit test; no external consumer needs the barrel export. This follows AC3.6's no-op path.
2. **Workflow sync mirrors role sync rather than generalizing.** `workflow-sync.ts` is intentionally parallel to `role-sync.ts`, with renamed discriminators and `workflowsErrors`. No shared abstraction was introduced.
3. **Workflow user edits are healthy in `overallOk`.** `syncAll()` treats workflow `user-modified` the same way the spec requires: preserved and not a failure. `source-missing` and `error` fail `overallOk`.
4. **Human renderer only prints captured stderr on nonzero exit.** This keeps successful reviews focused on stdout findings while surfacing failure stderr with a `stderr:` label. JSON mode remains unchanged.
5. **`docs/BACKLOG.md` untouched.** AC8 is strategist-owned after the corrected spec; builder made no admin-doc edits.
