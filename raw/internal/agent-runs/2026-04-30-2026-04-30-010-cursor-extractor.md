   That's the kind of silent drift the drift-prevention doctrine is for.

### Open questions for founder

The single decision: **where does Cursor put composer chat content, and how
should the extractor's contract reflect that?**

Three options laid out in the drift note
(`raw/internal/decisions/2026-04-30-DRIFT-cursor-chat-storage-location.md`).
Briefly:

- **Option 1 — Watch globalStorage; per-composer tracking.** Smallest change
  that actually captures chat. `metadata.workspace_id` becomes
  best-effort/nullable.
- **Option 2 — Watch globalStorage AND keep workspace mapping.** Preserves
  spec's metadata shape but adds an in-memory composerId→workspace index
  with race semantics.
- **Option 3 — Defer composer; capture only legacy `aiService.prompts` per
  workspace.** Honors the spec literally; misses what users actually do
  in Cursor.

My read is Option 1, but I am not making the call.
