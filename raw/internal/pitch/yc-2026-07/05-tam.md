All numbers carry sources; flags at bottom. Pricing anchor: $25–50/seat/mo (sits between Copilot Business $19 and Cursor Business $40 / Glean ~$50 reported — defensible mid-band for cross-tool leverage).

## Headline (top-down)

- Professional developers worldwide: **36.5M** (SlashData, early 2025), 84% using or planning AI tools (Stack Overflow 2025) → **~30M AI-tool-using devs** = SAM ceiling.
- Paid AI dev seats are already a **$15B+/yr category**: Copilot 4.7M paid subs (+75% YoY, MSFT FY26 Q2); Cursor **$2B+ ARR** (Feb 2026, $29.3B valuation); Claude Code **$2.5B+ run-rate** (early 2026, ~4% of public GitHub commits).
- AI agents market: $7.84B (2025) → **$52.6B (2030)**, 46.3% CAGR (MarketsandMarkets; three corroborating estimates $42–48B).
- At $300–600/seat/yr across 30M AI-tool devs: **$9–18B TAM** for the personal layer alone; team layer expands per-seat value toward the Glean band.

## Beachhead (bottom-up, the multi-hat cofounder)

- New company formation is accelerating *because of* AI: Stripe Atlas 23k incorporations in 2025, **Q1 2026 +130% YoY**; ~650 YC companies/yr; ~10,500 funded NA rounds/yr; AI captured 41.7% of US seed capital (Carta 2025). Solo founders at 36% and rising (Carta 2026) — the multi-hat pattern is growing structurally.
- Conservative beachhead math: **100k AI-native small teams × 2–5 seats × $25–50/mo ≈ $60–300M ARR wedge**, before expanding into AI-heavy dev teams at larger companies.
