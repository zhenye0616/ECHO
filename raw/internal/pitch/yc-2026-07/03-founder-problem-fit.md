- **Built ECHO with ECHO.** 111 backlog items shipped by a fleet of AI agents (Claude Code strategist, Codex builders/reviewers, Cursor) coordinating *through ECHO's own coord substrate* — cross-tool review queues, role-typed task state, atomic claims.
