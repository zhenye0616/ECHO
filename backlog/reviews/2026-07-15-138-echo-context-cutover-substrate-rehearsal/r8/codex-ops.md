Reserve an evidence path and schema admitted by root validation, with descriptor-relative durable append/commit semantics, so this failure remains operator-visible without making every later invocation reject newly created evidence as unknown root content."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC1 and AC7 rollback lifecycle"
    finding: "The exhaustive phase table has no normative state for an interrupted `active` to `rolled_back` operation. Before the flip, an intentional freeze or partial restoration conflicts with the `active` resume requirement to prove one new writer; after the flip, `rolled_back` immediately permits recutover even though old-full readiness is still unproven. Add durable rollback-in-progress and rollback-complete state, define cut reuse or invalidation and resume re-proofs, and forbid recutover until serialized post-flip readiness completion is committed."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC1, AC2, and AC7 rolled-back admission"
    finding: "The rollback authorization tuple binds old-stack restoration digests but does not require durable neutralization of the new context and residual start jobs or proof of zero new writers and listeners. A crash after the flip can therefore admit old full while a new-stack KeepAlive process remains runnable. Stop and disable the new jobs, prove zero new context and coordination writers/listeners before the flip, bind those results into rollback authorization, and cover the post-flip competing-restart race."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC2 residual entrypoint"
    finding: "Only rollback-full receives an explicit authority fence. The separately packaged residual entrypoint can otherwise be invoked directly or relaunched by KeepAlive in absent, planned, or rolled-back state, opening canonical coordination storage and binding its endpoint beside old-full authority. Give the residual entrypoint a closed phase-admission table enforced under the same root, receipt, package-identity, and `authority.lock` checks, with direct-start, stale-plist, and relaunch-race tests."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC5 approval records"
    finding: "An unkeyed integrity SHA-256 plus an unnamed item-139-only authorization binding does not define an authentication boundary: any caller can recompute an integrity hash. Pin the approval schema, cryptographic algorithm, verifier trust anchor, purpose and mode domain separation, signer custody boundary, and replay rules in this producer contract; test an unauthorized but internally consistent rehashed record in addition to simple tampering."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC5 deployment trust chain"
    finding: "The pre-extraction bootstrap is trusted by having that same executable verify a manifest containing its own hash, but substituted code executes before any self-check and can mutate or fake the result. Require a separately trusted item-139 launcher to authenticate the approval and bootstrap bytes before execution. The launched bootstrap must then hold no-follow regular-file descriptors for the manifest, approval, and archive through consumption and require canonical argv equality for the approval-bound prefix and root, with pathname-swap fixtures."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC5 deployment transaction"
    finding: "The bootstrap transaction is neither required to acquire the sole `authority.lock` nor given a final visibility marker checked by consumers. Concurrent bootstraps can interleave journal, receipt, and identity writes, while a crash after identity commit but before evidence provisioning leaves a receipt and identity pair the fence can accept as fully installed. Serialize the whole transaction on `authority.lock`, publish through journal-owned no-clobber staging, commit one completion record last, require it during startup, and test bootstrap/bootstrap, bootstrap/fence, bootstrap/execute, and every partial-state combination."
  - severity: "high"
    where: "backlog/proposed/2026-07-15-138-echo-context-cutover-substrate-rehearsal.md:AC4 per-target CAS"
    finding: "The transition table has no outcome for a crash after target rename but before the apply result is journaled: the target equals the after-image while the journal does not yet record application. It also defines no atomic commit and replay primitive for delete-type targets, because temporary-to-target rename cannot produce an absent after-image. Add a write-ahead intent binding the staged inode and hash, define pending-after reconciliation, and specify descriptor-pinned delete or tombstone plus parent-fsync semantics with kill tests."
