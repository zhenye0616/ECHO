# Felt, Not Seen

## Definition

The product is felt everywhere, lived in nowhere. ECHO is ambient by default and visible only on demand for trust. The daily experience is presence without a window; the user works, the AIs they use are smarter, and ECHO never pulls attention to itself.

## The Reference Design Is Electricity, Not Slack

You don't see electricity. You don't open it. You don't visit it. But every appliance works because it's there. The fuse box exists in your basement; you check it twice a year. The other 363 days, electricity is purely *experienced through the things it powers.* ECHO works the same way.

## How It Manifests in V1

- **Browser extension** → felt as smarter web AIs
- **MCP server** → felt as smarter desktop AIs
- **Hotkey overlay** → summoned for explicit moments, disappears
- **Audit page** → exists for trust, used a few minutes a month

Unifying principle: **the product appears in the result, not in the interface.**

## Implications

- **Marketing:** no screenshots of "the app" — there isn't one to screenshot. Show what users *do better* with it.
- **Onboarding:** install once, grant permissions, forget it's there. From that moment on, every AI is smarter. ~30-second tutorial.
- **What competitors can copy visually:** nothing. The work is in the substrate.

## The Trust Refinement

Pure invisibility kills trust for a memory product. The right form is **felt by default, visible on demand**. The audit page exists because:

- Users need to inspect what ECHO knows
- Users need to manage permissions
- Users need to forget specific memories

But the audit page is a *settings menu visited a few minutes a month*, not the daily experience.

## Why Incumbents Can't Copy This

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft cannot credibly ship a felt-not-seen product. Their business models depend on users *being in their app* — paying them, viewing their UI, building habit with their brand. Felt-not-seen is structurally available to ECHO because ECHO doesn't sell attention; it sells the disappearance of itself in service of the user's work.
